Privacy Policy

GolVazhe (گل‌واژه) · Last updated September 2026

GolVazhe is a Persian word-a-day app. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used, in plain terms.

What we collect

GolVazhe does not require an account and does not collect any personal information automatically. The only data we ever receive is what you choose to submit through the app's "Give Feedback" feature:

  • The message you write
  • Your email address, only if you choose to enter one (so we can reply) — this field is optional

This feedback is sent securely to our support inbox via Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree processes this submission on our behalf and does not use it for any other purpose. We do not sell, share, or use your feedback for advertising.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers or ad networks
  • No location data
  • No access to contacts, photos, or other device data

What stays on your device

Your chosen fluency level, starred words, and word progress are stored locally on your device (and shared with the GolVazhe home screen widget, if you've added one). This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

Word content

The app fetches its Persian vocabulary list from a public data file we host on GitHub. This is a one-way, anonymous content download — no information about you or your device is sent as part of it.

Children's privacy

GolVazhe does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require any personal information to use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the "Give Feedback" option in the app's Settings tab — that's the fastest way to reach us.
