Privacy Policy

GolVazhe (گل‌واژه) · Last updated September 2026

GolVazhe is a Persian word-a-day app. Here's what the app collects and why, in plain language.

What we collect

You don't need an account to use GolVazhe. We collect two things:

  • Feedback you send us. If you use "Give Feedback" in Settings, we get your message. If you choose to type in your email, we get that too, so we can reply. The email field is optional.
  • Basic usage counts. We keep simple totals of things like how many times the app is opened, a word's detail page is viewed, the fluency level is changed, or the widget is tapped. These are just numbers. Nothing ties a count to you, your name, or your device.

Feedback messages go to our inbox through Formspree, a third-party form service that handles the message on our behalf. We don't sell your feedback, share it, or use it for advertising.

What we don't collect

  • No account or login
  • No name, unless you type one into a feedback message
  • No advertising identifiers or ad networks
  • No location data
  • No access to contacts, photos, or other device data
  • No way to trace usage counts back to a specific person

What stays on your device

Your fluency level, starred words, and word progress are stored on your phone, and shared with the GolVazhe home screen widget if you've added one. This never leaves your device. We can't see it.

Word content

The app downloads its word list from a public file we host on GitHub. This is just a content download. Nothing about you or your device goes along with it.

Children's privacy

GolVazhe doesn't ask for, or knowingly collect, personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use "Give Feedback" in the app's Settings tab. That's the fastest way to reach us.
