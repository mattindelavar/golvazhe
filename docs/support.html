GolVazhe Support

GolVazhe (گل‌واژه) teaches a new Persian word every day, with a dictionary of past words and a home screen widget.

Getting help or sending feedback

The fastest way to reach us is directly in the app:

1. Open GolVazhe

2. Go to the Settings tab

3. Tap Give Feedback

Include your email if you'd like a reply — it's optional.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. GolVazhe works fully offline after the first launch, with no sign-in required.

Why does the widget still show yesterday's word?
Reopen the app briefly — this refreshes the widget's data. If it persists, try removing and re-adding the widget.

Can I change my fluency level?
Yes — Settings → Fluency Level. Each level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) has its own independent sequence of words.

Where does the word list come from?
Words are curated and periodically updated; the app checks for new words automatically.
